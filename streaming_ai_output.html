New on Streaming

The streaming calendar is packed with fresh arrivals this week, bringing everything from glossy romantic escapism and sharp-edged crime drama to true-crime deep dives, reality competitions, travel series, and offbeat comedy. Whether you are in the mood for a dark mystery, a comfort-watch romance, or a new unscripted obsession, there is plenty to add to your queue.

This week’s lineup leans especially strong on television, with new docuseries, relationship experiments, competition formats, and character-driven dramas arriving alongside a handful of notable movie releases. From sun-drenched murder in Spain to Los Angeles noir, from sports trivia to cocktail craft, the new streaming slate has something for nearly every entertainment mood.

Here is your ShowBiz guide to the latest movies and TV shows arriving on major streaming platforms.

20/20 Britain's Most Notorious Crime Investigations

Type: TV

Release Date: July 30, 2026

This true-crime docuseries revisits six of the United Kingdom’s most high-profile criminal cases, including shocking scandals, deadly chases, and killers hidden in plain sight. Viewers who appreciate investigative storytelling should watch for its character-driven approach and the weight of the “20/20” news brand, which brings a polished journalistic lens to gripping real-world mysteries.

Benidorm Is Murder

Type: TV

Release Date: July 30, 2026

A former UK detective trying to live quietly in Benidorm is pulled back into murder investigations when tourists begin turning up dead. With a crime-drama-obsessed barmaid as his unlikely partner, this series offers a lively mix of mystery, humor, and holiday chaos. Watch it if you like murder cases served with sunshine, banter, and a reluctant sleuth who cannot quite escape the job.

One Pace

Type: TV

Release Date: July 30, 2026

This long-running fan project re-edits the “One Piece” anime to more closely match the pacing of Eiichiro Oda’s manga. For fans who want a tighter viewing experience, it is a streamlined way to revisit the saga with a rhythm that stays closer to the source material. Watch it if you love the world of “One Piece” but prefer a more focused version of the journey.

Paris is Always a Good Idea

Type: TV

Release Date: July 30, 2026

Chelsea Martin’s trip to Paris for a major nonprofit donation becomes a deeply personal journey when a message from her late mother sends her back through the great loves she left behind. With stops in Ireland, Paris, and Spain, this romantic drama promises scenic escapism and emotional reflection. Watch it for the blend of travel, romance, and the timeless question of whether looking back can help someone move forward.

ESPN Jeopardy!

Type: TV

Release Date: July 31, 2026

This sports-themed spin-off brings ESPN personalities to the Alex Trebek Stage for a battle of knowledge, timing, and competitive pride. It is a natural pick for viewers who love trivia and sports culture in equal measure. Watch it to see familiar ESPN talent trade the broadcast desk for the buzzer in a format built for quick wit and high-stakes bragging rights.

Fightland

Type: TV

Release Date: July 31, 2026

A disgraced former boxing champion returns to London after incarceration, determined to take revenge on the crime syndicate he believes betrayed him. With its mix of boxing grit, underworld tension, and personal reckoning, “Fightland” looks built for viewers who want a hard-hitting drama with emotional stakes. Watch it for a revenge story where every punch carries history.

Rhythm of Bhutan

Type: TV

Release Date: July 31, 2026

This music selection show follows artists competing for the chance to become Bhutan’s first representative at the debut Eurovision Song Contest Asia. Across five episodes, the competition builds toward a grand final decided by jury and viewers. Watch it for rising talent, national pride, and the excitement of a historic first on an international stage.

L.O.L. Surprise! The Next Headliner

Type: TV

Release Date: August 01, 2026

Pop icon Diva Geneva needs the ultimate opening act for her worldwide Festival Tour, and five talented girls are chosen to live together, record music, master choreography, and compete for the opportunity. This is a bright, performance-driven series for younger viewers and families who enjoy music, ambition, and big-stage dreams. Watch it for the energy of a pop competition with friendship, talent, and sparkle at its center.

Richard E. Grant's Very Modern Odyssey

Type: TV

Release Date: August 02, 2026

Richard E. Grant retraces the route of Odysseus’ legendary 20-year voyage, traveling through Malta, Sicily, Italy, Tunisia, Turkey, Corfu, and Ithaca. Timed around renewed interest in “The Odyssey,” this travel series blends classical mythology with modern destinations. Watch it for culture, history, scenery, and Grant’s distinctive curiosity as he explores the places that inspired Homer’s epic.

Joe & David's Magical Sitcom Tour

Type: TV

Release Date: August 04, 2026

Joe Wilkinson and David Earl hit the road for a series that is part travelogue, part buddy comedy, and part loving tribute to classic British sitcoms. It is a warm pick for comedy fans who enjoy nostalgia with a loose, conversational feel. Watch it for two performers celebrating the shows that shaped British comedy while bringing their own chemistry to the journey.

SPECIAL OPS TRUE

Type: TV

Release Date: August 04, 2026

This documentary series revisits missions remembered around the world through firsthand testimony and never-before-seen footage. Rather than focusing only on the headlines, it centers the people who lived through the operations and remember every second. Watch it for an intense, inside-the-room perspective on high-pressure events where decisions carried life-or-death consequences.

The Cocktail Lab

Type: TV

Release Date: August 04, 2026

Hosted by Joe Gitter, this practical and entertaining series teaches viewers how to make better drinks at home, from martinis and Manhattans to highballs, sours, tropical cocktails, and zero-proof favorites. With lessons on ice, dilution, ratios, shaking, stirring, and batch preparation, it is a must-watch for home entertainers. Watch it if you want to upgrade your next gathering with real technique and a little bar-side confidence.

How to Catch a Dirtbag

Type: TV

Release Date: August 05, 2026

Michael McWhorter, also known as Tizzy Ent, sets out to find people who behave badly in public and think they can get away with it. The premise taps directly into the internet-age appetite for accountability, confrontation, and social investigation. Watch it if you are drawn to real-world misconduct stories and the tension of watching bad behavior get exposed.

Let’s Marry Harry

Type: TV

Release Date: August 05, 2026

Harry Jowsey’s search for a life partner comes with a twist: prospective brides must also impress his closest confidants, Amanda Kloots, Georgia Hassarati, and Sonny Henty. With friends helping make the tough calls, the show adds a fresh layer of scrutiny to the reality romance formula. Watch it for dating drama, personality clashes, and the question of whether a famous player can truly become husband material.

Sisters of Summerhill

Type: TV

Release Date: August 05, 2026

Three outspoken, big-hearted women reunite in their Georgia hometown and discover that saving the neighborhood may be easier than surviving each other. This comedy leans into friendship, family, hometown chaos, and the kind of wild schemes that test even the strongest bonds. Watch it for sharp humor, community spirit, and a trio whose love for one another is as loud as their disagreements.

Sterling Point

Type: TV

Release Date: August 05, 2026

Annie and her twin brother, raised in New York by their single father, discover that their estranged grandfather has left them an island on a lake. That inheritance sets up a story filled with family history, new beginnings, and the promise of secrets waiting to surface. Watch it if you like dramas about identity, legacy, and siblings pulled into a world they never expected to inherit.

The Shards

Type: TV

Release Date: August 05, 2026

Set in Los Angeles in 1981, this series follows a 17-year-old version of Bret Easton Ellis during his final year at the elite Buckley prep school. The arrival of mysterious student Robert Mallory coincides with the disturbing activities of a serial killer known as The Trawler. Watch it for an unsettling blend of coming-of-age drama, privileged-school tension, and slow-burn psychological menace.

D.O.P.E. Unit

Type: TV

Release Date: August 06, 2026

After the murder of a police chief’s son, a covert off-the-books task force is created to infiltrate Philadelphia’s most dangerous crime families. As the investigation deepens, the line between cop and criminal begins to vanish. Watch it for a gritty crime drama where loyalty is unstable, morality is compromised, and every alliance comes with a cost.

Monsters of God

Type: TV

Release Date: August 06, 2026

This docuseries follows Eric Goode into the underworld of exotic animal smuggling, beginning with reptile-world figures Hank Molt and Tommy Crutchfield. What starts as a story of rare animals, zoos, and illegal trade turns into a tale of rivalry, prison, revenge, and lasting consequences. Watch it for a stranger-than-fiction descent into a niche world where obsession and greed become dangerous.

Age Inappropriate

Type: TV

Release Date: August 07, 2026

This reality series explores extreme age-gap relationships, following couples at very different life stages as they try to prove their love is genuine. It is designed for viewers who are fascinated by complicated romances, social judgment, and unconventional relationship dynamics. Watch it for emotional conflict, uncomfortable questions, and the tension between public perception and private commitment.

DC

Type: Movie

Release Date: August 07, 2026

Rebel Devadas teams with Chandra in a dangerous world where passion and peril collide, forcing them into a turbulent alliance built on survival, bloodshed, and tested loyalties. This movie promises a charged mix of action, intensity, and emotional stakes. Watch it if you want a high-risk story where trust is fragile and survival demands sacrifice.

Ice Cream Man

Type: Movie

Release Date: August 07, 2026

An idyllic summer town spirals into madness when an ice cream man serves children sweet treats with horrifying consequences. The contrast between nostalgic summer imagery and unsettling terror gives this horror movie an instantly creepy hook. Watch it if you enjoy sinister small-town stories that turn something innocent into a nightmare.

One Night Only

Type: Movie

Release Date: August 07, 2026

Two love-starved strangers collide on the one night of the year when single people are allowed to have sex. With a provocative premise and a focus on loneliness, desire, and connection, this movie offers more than a simple romantic setup. Watch it for an adult, high-concept romance that asks what intimacy means when the rules of the world change for one night only.

Super Troopers 3

Type: Movie

Release Date: August 07, 2026

Farva’s outrageous engagement to Thorny’s sister sends the Super Troopers into wedding chaos, family scheming, and a new drug-ring investigation. The sequel brings back the franchise’s taste for absurd situations, unruly characters, and comedy built on escalating disaster. Watch it if you are ready for another round of law-enforcement mayhem where saving the day and saving the wedding may be equally impossible.
