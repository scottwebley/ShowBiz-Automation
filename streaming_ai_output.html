New on Streaming

Streaming queues are getting a serious refresh, with a new wave of TV premieres, documentaries, genre swings, food-world rescues, true-crime investigations, and big-ticket movie arrivals landing for entertainment fans looking for their next obsession.

This week’s lineup has something for almost every mood: reality competition, historical deep dives, adult animation, sports stories, dystopian drama, digital horror, political sci-fi comedy, and high-stakes thrillers. Whether you want comfort viewing, conversation-starting documentaries, or a blockbuster-scale movie night, these are the new titles to keep on your radar.

Type: TV

Release Date: July 23, 2026

Type: TV
