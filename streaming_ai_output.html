New on Streaming

The streaming calendar is heating up with a packed lineup of fresh television, true crime, food competition, genre thrills, documentaries, animation, and blockbuster-sized movie arrivals. Whether you are looking for a comfort-watch reality series, a tense mystery, a sharp sci-fi escape, or a major superhero event, this week’s new releases offer plenty to add to your queue.

From celebrity dinner parties and restaurant rescues to historical deep dives, dystopian drama, sports stories, and high-stakes crime, the latest wave of streaming titles brings something for every mood. There are also several standout movie arrivals, including action, intrigue, period suspense, and a much-anticipated return to the world of Spider-Man.

Here is your ShowBiz guide to the new movies and television shows arriving on streaming, with a quick look at why each one deserves your attention.

Come Dine With Me: Celebrity Philippines

Type: TV

Release Date: July 23, 2026

This celebrity-hosted dinner party competition brings food, personality, and friendly rivalry to the table. Viewers who enjoy lighthearted reality television should tune in for the mix of home entertaining, playful scoring, and famous faces competing for charity.
