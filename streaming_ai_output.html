New on Streaming

Streaming’s latest wave is packed with true crime, sharp comedy, high-stakes drama, destination romance, competition energy, and a few genre swings built for viewers who like their watchlists unpredictable. Whether you are in the mood for a gripping investigation, a feel-good sitcom, a chilling summer horror story, or a glossy relationship experiment, this lineup offers plenty to sample.

This week’s arrivals lean into variety: prestige-style documentary storytelling, scripted thrillers with dark secrets, travel-led television, music competition, reality romance, and movie-night picks ranging from action to comedy to horror. Here are the new movies and television shows worth adding to your queue.
