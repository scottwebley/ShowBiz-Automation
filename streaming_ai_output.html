New on Streaming

Streaming shelves are getting a fresh refresh, and this week’s arrivals cover plenty of ground: globe-trotting travel, sharp reality competition, true-crime documentary, high-concept sci-fi, animated chaos, prestige drama, and a few genre swings designed to get fans talking.

Whether you are looking for a comfort-watch travel series, a buzzy franchise return, a dark comedy with bite, or a thriller with apocalyptic stakes, these new movies and shows offer a wide spread of viewing moods. Here is what entertainment fans should have on their radar.

Oti and Richard’s South African Odyssey

Type: TV

Release Date: July 21, 2026

Oti Mabuse returns to South Africa with Reverend Richard Coles for a personal and scenic homecoming journey. Viewers should watch for the warmth of its travelogue format, the chemistry between its hosts, and the chance to experience South Africa through a deeply personal lens.
