New on Streaming

The streaming calendar is serving up a packed slate of fresh arrivals, from high-concept comedies and true-crime deep dives to reality competitions, travel escapes, supernatural horror, and character-driven dramas. Whether you are chasing a weekend binge, a conversation-starting documentary, or a new guilty pleasure, this week’s lineup has plenty to keep your watchlist busy.

What stands out is the variety: celebrities going back to school, best friends on the run, restaurants fighting for survival, mysteries unfolding in outback mines and quiet college towns, and even a multiverse crisis sparked by a broken device. For entertainment fans, the fun is in choosing the mood — outrageous, eerie, emotional, or escapist.

Here is ShowBiz’s guide to the new movies and television shows arriving on streaming, with a closer look at why each one deserves your attention.

Nation's Dumbest

Type: TV

Release Date: July 15, 2026

A celebrity competition series with a deliciously chaotic twist, this one turns the usual win-at-all-costs format upside down. With Jack Whitehall hosting the school-themed mayhem, viewers can expect pop quizzes, report cards, recess-style drama, and plenty of public humiliation. Watch it for a light, rowdy reality fix where being the best is not exactly the goal.

Ride or Die

Type: TV

Release Date: July 15, 2026

This action-comedy mystery puts friendship under extreme pressure when one best friend discovers the other has been hiding a life as a highly trained assassin. The hook is instantly bingeable: secrets, betrayal, danger, and a mysterious enemy closing in. Watch it for the mix of explosive stakes and emotional repair as Judith and Debbie fight to survive together.

The Baddie Rap Show

Type: TV

Release Date: July 15, 2026

This reality competition dives into the high-pressure world of female battle rap, where raw talent is just the beginning. Weekly challenges promise shifting alliances, sharpened verses, and dramatic tests of confidence. Watch it for big personalities, lyrical showdowns, and the thrill of performers trying to prove they belong in a cutthroat arena.

MLP Mayhem

Type: TV

Release Date: July 16, 2026

Built around Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling, this title is aimed squarely at fans who love the spectacle, athleticism, and larger-than-life energy of the ring. Watch it if your streaming tastes lean toward hard-hitting rivalries, showmanship, and the unpredictable pulse of live-event-style entertainment.

Scott Peterson: The New Evidence

Type: TV

Release Date: July 16, 2026

This documentary revisits one of America’s most polarizing high-profile cases through the lens of newly surfaced evidence. Led by veteran defense attorney and ABC News legal consultant Chris Pixley, it offers a detailed re-examination of the investigation. Watch it for a serious, forensic approach to a case that continues to generate debate.

The Chase: Around the World

Type: TV

Release Date: July 16, 2026

The beloved quiz-show format expands into a global spin-off, taking the familiar thrill of fast answers and high-stakes pursuit to a broader stage. Watch it for brainy competition, international scale, and the kind of nail-biting trivia tension that makes quiz shows so addictive.

The Hawk

Type: TV

Release Date: July 16, 2026

Golf legend Lonnie “The Hawk” Hawkins is chasing one final major, but the real drama may come from the chaos he brings to everyone around him. This looks like a sports-world character piece with personal stakes, comic friction, and legacy on the line. Watch it for the pressure of a comeback story mixed with family and career turmoil.

The Reclaimers: Made in Suffolk

Type: TV

Release Date: July 16, 2026

Set at Tinkers Barn in the Suffolk countryside, this series follows three generations of the Warnes family as they rescue, restore, and repurpose forgotten treasures. It is ideal comfort viewing for fans of salvage, restoration, and family-run businesses. Watch it for the satisfaction of seeing discarded objects given a second life.

Ann Droid

Type: TV

Release Date: July 17, 2026

Starring Diane Morgan and Sue Johnston, this comedy starts with a nightmare scenario: an overly attentive, socially awkward, second-hand robot carer. The premise sets up a sharp clash between human irritation and artificial helpfulness. Watch it for offbeat humor, odd-couple energy, and the promise of a droid who may be more useful than expected.

Backrooms: INCEPTION

Type: TV

Release Date: July 17, 2026

Set in 1997, this eerie series follows Colton Bond, a mute man obsessed with exploring “The Backrooms” and uncovering their origins. The twist is ominous: he may not be the only one searching. Watch it for creeping dread, liminal-space horror, and a mystery built around a place that seems to have been waiting for him.

Surgery Secrets of the Super Rich

Type: TV

Release Date: July 17, 2026

This docu-style series steps inside the world of elite cosmetic procedures, from lunchtime lifts to bespoke tweakments with staggering price tags. Watch it for a glossy, revealing look at the doctors, clients, and luxury beauty culture behind the super-rich pursuit of perfection.

The F Ward

Type: TV

Release Date: July 17, 2026

Failed medical interns get one last chance at Sydney’s underfunded Pines Hospital, where personal flaws and past mistakes are as urgent as the cases they face. Watch it for a hospital-set story with underdog spirit, workplace chaos, and characters trying to prove they can still become doctors.

The Hairdresser Mysteries

Type: TV

Release Date: July 17, 2026

High-end hairdresser Lily Petal leaves the competitive city scene behind for a small village shop on a cobbled street. The setup has all the ingredients for cozy intrigue: a fresh start, a close-knit community, and secrets waiting beneath the surface. Watch it if you enjoy lighter mysteries with charm, style, and village atmosphere.

Young Farts Trailer Parts

Type: TV

Release Date: July 17, 2026

Small-town brothers Jagger and Dawson run a booming RV parts business, turning trailer trash into cash while chasing outrageous schemes and chaotic stunts. Watch it for big personalities, family loyalty, and a reality-comedy rhythm built on wild ideas and blue-collar hustle.

Treasure & Dirt

Type: TV

Release Date: July 19, 2026

A headless torso found deep inside an opal mine sends Ivan Lucic and Nell Buchanan to the desolate outback mining town of Nullah, where law and order is anything but guaranteed. Watch it for a gritty mystery, a striking setting, and the tense atmosphere of a community where secrets are buried as deep as the mines.

Oti and Richard’s South African Odyssey

Type: TV

Release Date: July 21, 2026

Oti Mabuse returns to South Africa with Reverend Richard Coles for a heartfelt journey through her homeland. This travel series promises a personal homecoming as much as a scenic adventure. Watch it for culture, companionship, emotional discovery, and the pleasure of seeing South Africa through a meaningful personal lens.

Fake Hundreds

Type: Movie

Release Date: July 23, 2026

After receiving a cancer diagnosis, a man attempts to confront God, setting up a story with intimate spiritual and emotional stakes. Watch it if you are drawn to films that grapple with mortality, faith, anger, and the search for meaning when life suddenly changes.

Pompeii: Out of Time with Tom Hiddleston

Type: TV

Release Date: July 23, 2026

This historical exploration revisits the eruption of Vesuvius in 79 A.D. and the astonishingly preserved Roman city it left behind. With Tom Hiddleston attached, the journey offers star power alongside ancient history. Watch it for a dramatic, accessible look at one of the world’s most haunting archaeological stories.

Restaurant Impossible: Last Call

Type: TV

Release Date: July 23, 2026

This reboot and spinoff brings a fresh approach to restaurant rescues, with chef Aarón Sánchez and restaurateur Jen Agg helping struggling businesses on a ticking clock and limited budget. Watch it for high-pressure makeovers, modern industry insight, and the emotional stakes of owners fighting to keep their dreams alive.

Stuart Fails to Save the Universe

Type: TV

Release Date: July 23, 2026

Comic book store owner Stuart Bloom accidentally triggers a multiverse Armageddon after breaking a device built by Sheldon and Leonard. With Denise, Bert, and Barry Kripke joining the mission to restore reality, this series leans into nerd-culture chaos and cosmic comedy. Watch it for a playful, high-stakes spin on familiar personalities and science-fiction absurdity.

The Idaho Murders: A College Town Nightmare

Type: TV

Release Date: July 23, 2026

This documentary examines the 2022 murders of four University of Idaho students in Moscow, Idaho, and the investigation that followed. It focuses on a quiet college town shaken by fear, unanswered questions, and lasting trauma. Watch it for a sobering true-crime account of a case that deeply affected a community.

Her Private Hell

Type: Movie

Release Date: July 24, 2026

A mysterious mist consumes a futuristic metropolis, unleashing a deadly entity as a troubled young woman searches for her father. Her journey collides with an American GI trying to rescue his daughter from Hell. Watch it for dystopian horror, emotional urgency, and a grim odyssey through a city swallowed by nightmare.

Motor City

Type: Movie

Release Date: July 24, 2026

Set in 1970s Detroit, this revenge drama follows John Miller after he falls for a gangster’s girl, is sent to prison for a crime he did not commit, and emerges with payback on his mind. Watch it for crime-story grit, period atmosphere, and the classic pull of a wronged man taking aim at the person who destroyed his life.

Pinocchio: Unstrung

Type: Movie

Release Date: July 24, 2026

This dark reimagining turns the familiar puppet tale into something far more sinister, as Pinocchio, influenced by a sinister Cricket and created by Geppetto, sets out on a violent crusade to become a real boy. Watch it for fairy-tale horror, twisted mythology, and a brutal new spin on a classic character.
