From family adventures to bruising action thrillers and summer horror, the theatrical slate has plenty of reasons to get back in front of the big screen. Here are the ShowBiz picks to see now, plus the titles worth keeping on your radar.

🎬 Editor's Pick

Genre: Animated Adventure

Pixar’s beloved toy-box saga returns with a smartly timed new threat: screen obsession. With Buzz, Woody, Jessie and the gang facing a digital rival for playtime, this is the rare sequel that feels both nostalgic and sharply current.
