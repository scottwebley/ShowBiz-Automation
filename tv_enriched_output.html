🔥 Best TV Shows Right Now

President Curtis

Genre: Animated Sci-Fi Comedy

This eccentric political sci-fi comedy turns the White House into a launchpad for interdimensional diplomacy, paranormal crises and absurd executive chaos. Fans of offbeat adult animation should find plenty to enjoy in its surreal, high-concept premise.

▶ Watch Official Trailer



Force of Nature

Genre: Nature Documentary

A visually driven wildlife series with urgent contemporary relevance, Force of Nature follows animals adapting to climate extremes across the globe. Viewers who love immersive natural-history storytelling will be drawn to its mix of spectacle, science and survival.



Modern Marvels: WWII

Genre: History Documentary

For history buffs and engineering-minded viewers, this WWII-focused installment examines the machines, weapons and innovations that helped define the conflict. Its appeal lies in connecting battlefield technology to the world it helped shape.

Game Day Murders

Genre: True Crime

Hosted by Lindsay Czarniak and backed by Shaquille O’Neal as executive producer, Game Day Murders digs into shocking real-life cases tied to the sports world. It is built for viewers who like true crime with a compelling cultural hook.



The Idaho Murders: College Nightmare

Genre: True Crime Documentary

This chilling true-crime series revisits one of the most devastating recent murder cases, balancing the investigation with attention to the four young lives lost. It is a serious, detail-driven watch for audiences following major contemporary cases.

House of Stassi

Genre: Reality TV

Stassi Schroeder returns to the spotlight in a series built around reinvention, reputation and the chaos of a complicated inner circle. Reality fans who like personality-driven drama with a pop-culture edge should keep this one close.

▶ Watch Official Trailer



Paris is Always a Good Idea

Genre: Romantic Drama

A globe-trotting romantic journey from Ireland to Paris to Spain, this series follows a driven executive confronting love, memory and second chances. It is an inviting pick for viewers who want escapist scenery with emotional stakes.

▶ Watch Official Trailer



Benidorm Is Murder

Genre: Mystery Comedy

A former detective turned bar owner is pulled back into crime-solving when tourists start dying in paradise. With a sunny setting and a crime-drama superfan as his unlikely partner, it offers a lighter spin on the murder-mystery format.

ESPN Jeopardy!

Genre: Game Show

This sports-themed Jeopardy! spin-off brings ESPN personalities to the iconic quiz stage, turning athletic trivia into a fast, familiar competition. It is an easy recommendation for sports fans and game-show loyalists alike.



Fightland

Genre: Crime Drama

A disgraced boxing champion returns to London seeking vengeance against the syndicate he believes betrayed him. The setup promises bruising underworld drama, personal stakes and a lead character fighting for more than redemption.
