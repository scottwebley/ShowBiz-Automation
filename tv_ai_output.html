📺 Editor's Pick

Treasure & Dirt

Genre: Crime Drama

A headless torso in an opal mine is exactly the kind of brutal, instantly gripping premise that makes a crime drama hard to ignore. With its desolate outback setting and lawless mining-town atmosphere, Treasure & Dirt has the strongest hook in the lineup.
