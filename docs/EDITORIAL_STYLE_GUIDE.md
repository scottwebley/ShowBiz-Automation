# ShowBiz Editorial Style Guide

Version 1.0

---

# Mission

ShowBiz exists to cover the entire entertainment industry with accurate, original, engaging journalism.

Readers come first.

SEO comes second.

Automation exists to improve consistency, not replace editorial judgment.

---

# Brand Voice

ShowBiz should sound like a modern entertainment magazine.

Professional.

Energetic.

Knowledgeable.

Never sensational simply to attract clicks.

Never robotic.

Never academic.

---

# Writing Style

Write naturally.

Use short paragraphs.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Explain industry terms when needed.

Assume the reader enjoys entertainment but is not necessarily an industry expert.

---

# Headlines

Headlines should be informative first.

Avoid clickbait.

Avoid ALL CAPS.

Avoid excessive punctuation.

Bad

YOU WON'T BELIEVE THIS!!!!

Good

Why Christopher Nolan's Next Film Is Already Generating Oscar Buzz

---

# Introductions

The first paragraph should answer:

What happened?

Why does it matter?

Why should the reader continue?

---

# Tone

Confident.

Friendly.

Professional.

Curious.

Never sarcastic.

Never mean.

Never preachy.

---

# Original Articles

Original articles should:

Teach

Explain

Entertain

Inform

Readers should finish the article knowing something they did not know before.

---

# Entertainment Coverage

ShowBiz covers:

Movies

Television

Streaming

Music

Concerts

Broadway

Fashion

Modeling

Gaming

Celebrity Business

Entertainment Technology

Awards

Industry News

No area of entertainment should feel secondary.

---

# AI Writing Rules

Every article should read as if written by an experienced entertainment journalist.

Never mention AI.

Never mention prompts.

Never mention automation.

---

# Internal Links

Internal links should be relevant.

Never force links.

Prefer quality over quantity.

---

# Images

Images should support the story.

Never distract.

Official artwork is preferred.

Avoid unrelated stock photos.

---

# Guide Pages

Guide pages should help readers make decisions.

Movies

"What should I see?"

TV

"What should I watch?"

Streaming

"What's worth streaming?"

Concerts

"Which tours should I attend?"

Every guide should answer a reader's question.

---

# Homepage

The homepage should feel alive.

Top stories should change as news changes.

Older stories should naturally rotate downward.

---

# Editorial Principles

Accuracy before speed.

Originality before SEO.

Readers before algorithms.

Consistency before experimentation.

Quality before quantity.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Build the most trusted independent entertainment newsroom powered by intelligent automation.

Automation should increase quality, not reduce it.

Every improvement should make the reader experience better.
